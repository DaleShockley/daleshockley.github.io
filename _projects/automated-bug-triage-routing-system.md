An org-wide rollout of an agentic AI bug-triage tool built to cut the time engineers spent manually reading, classifying, and routing incoming defects.

The system connects to Jira, GitHub, and MCP to read new issues as they come in, classify them, and either resolve them automatically or route them to the right team with the right context attached. Rolled out across Intuit's engineering org, it cut defect triage time by 89% and resolved 82% of incoming defects automatically, with results briefed directly to engineering and program leadership.
